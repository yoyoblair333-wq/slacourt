How a claim is decided

  1. File an incident window (UTC) on an SLA, optionally with one incident page from a registered status-page host.
  2. Assess: every validator fetches the page itself and reduces it to {verdict, overlaps, minutes (rounded down to 15), severity}. They must match the leader exactly, or there is no decision.
  3. Challenge window: the customer or provider can appeal once; the round runs again.
  4. Finalize: the credit (schedule % of the monthly fee) moves from the pool to the customer. Unreadable or unclear pages pay nothing.
